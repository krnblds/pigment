# Cyberpunk alley decals

RGBA PNGs with transparent backgrounds, 1024 px on the long side. Generated by `tools/make_decals.py` (Pillow + Windows fonts); rerun it to regenerate.
The neon files are plain white glyph shapes with no glow, so you can tint them and use them as emissive masks. The renderer adds the glow.

- `neon_ramen.png` (1024x268): white filled katakana "ラーメン" for a ramen shop neon sign; tint it and use it as emission.
- `neon_dennou.png` (517x1024): white vertical kanji "電脳" ("cyber-brain") for a tall hanging neon sign.
- `neon_noodles_24h.png` (1024x169): white outline-tube "NOODLES 24H" (bent glass look) for the shop-front neon.
- `neon_bar.png` (1024x454): white outline-tube "BAR" neon sign above a doorway.
- `neon_open.png` (1024x337): white outline-tube "OPEN" neon sign for a window.
- `logo_hoshikawa_dynamics.png` (1024x380): logo for the invented "Hoshikawa Dynamics" corporation: red hex mark, white wordmark and 星川ダイナミクス株式会社. Use it on vending machines, crates and AC units.
- `warning_high_voltage.png` (1024x576): black-on-yellow "DANGER HIGH VOLTAGE 高電圧 危険 立入禁止" plate with a lightning bolt, for electrical boxes and AC units.
- `hazard_triangle.png` (1024x1003): yellow and black warning triangle with an exclamation mark, a generic hazard sticker.
- `stencil_sector_7g.png` (1024x209): spray-stencil "SECTOR 7-G" with overspray speckle, painted on walls, dumpsters and barrels.
- `label_barcode_serial.png` (1024x486): asset tag with a Code39 barcode (HK-7G-0451), serial and spec lines, for machinery and crates.
- `graffiti_tag_kaze.png` (1024x593): "KAZE" graffiti tag with a yellow-to-pink fill, dark outline, cyan drop shadow and drips, for walls and dumpsters.
- `graffiti_tag_zer0.png` (1024x871): "ZER0" graffiti tag with a green-to-blue fill, white outline, purple shadow and heavy drips, for walls and shutters.
- `poster_yume.png` (720x1024): opaque "DREAMLINK" neural-implant ad poster with a big 夢 kanji, diagonal neon bars and small EN/JP text blocks, for wall posters.
